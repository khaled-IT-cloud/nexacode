How a project runs

Four stages, roughly six to ten days depending on scope. You'll know what's happening at each one.

01

Discovery

We learn your business, your customers, and what the site actually needs to do for you.

02

Design

We design pages around how your customers think and browse, not a generic template.

03

Build

We write clean, tested code — the same standard we'd hold for any production software.

04

Launch & support

We ship it, watch how it performs, and stay on to fix and extend it as you grow.
